30/60/90 SaaS Onboarding

A repeatable onboarding model built around time-to-value rather than task completion.

Case Study Disclaimer: This project uses simulated data and scenarios to demonstrate my methodology, decision-making, and approach to solving real-world customer success challenges.

0-30

Kickoff, stakeholder map, success criteria, configuration, training and first meaningful workflow.

31-60

Validate adoption, remove friction and enable secondary workflows.

61-90

Review value evidence, establish ongoing cadence and agree next priorities.

Measures

Time to first value, activation, blockers, stakeholder participation and adoption trend.

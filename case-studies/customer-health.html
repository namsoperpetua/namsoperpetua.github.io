INDEPENDENT PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY

Customer Health & Renewal Risk

A decision system for knowing where a CSM should intervene before renewal pressure arrives.

Case Study Disclaimer: This project uses simulated data and scenarios to demonstrate my methodology, decision-making, and approach to solving real-world customer success challenges.

Business problem

A growing book of business needs an explainable way to distinguish healthy accounts from accounts quietly losing value.

Model

Adoption 30%, engagement 20%, support friction 20%, outcome progress 30%. The score is a signal, not the decision.

Cadence

Weekly risk review for red accounts, monthly review for watch accounts, and documented recovery plans with owner, milestone and follow-up date.

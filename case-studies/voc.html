Voice of Customer System

A closed-loop system for turning customer conversations into credible product evidence.

Case Study Disclaimer: This project uses simulated data and scenarios to demonstrate my methodology, decision-making, and approach to solving real-world customer success challenges.

Workflow

Capture → normalize → categorize → quantify → validate → prioritize → communicate → close the loop.

Evidence

Requests are stronger when they appear across customers, map to a meaningful workflow, create measurable friction and align strategically.

Output

Theme, evidence, affected segment, problem statement, impact, proposed next step and status.

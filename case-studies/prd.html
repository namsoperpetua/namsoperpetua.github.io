INDEPENDENT PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY

Product Requirements Document

A customer-friction problem translated into testable product requirements.

Case Study Disclaimer: This project uses simulated data and scenarios to demonstrate my methodology, decision-making, and approach to solving real-world customer success challenges.

Problem

Users repeatedly need support assistance to complete a recurring workflow.

Goal

Increase self-service completion while preserving clarity and controls.

Requirements

Clear entry, fewer unnecessary steps, contextual guidance, understandable errors and event instrumentation.

Rollout

Pilot, measure completion/support signals, resolve material edge cases, then expand.
